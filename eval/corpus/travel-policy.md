# Solaris Dynamics - Business Travel Policy (2026 edition)

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Solaris Dynamics travelling on company
business. Approval from the direct manager is required before booking any trip.

## Flights

- Economy class is mandatory for flights shorter than 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights between 6 and 10 hours.
- Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 10 hours and requires approval from a
  vice president.
- Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever possible.

## Trains

Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours. First class is permitted for
journeys longer than 2 hours.

## Accommodation

Hotel costs are capped at 180 EUR per night in most cities. In London, Paris, Zurich and
New York the cap is 260 EUR per night. Breakfast is reimbursed if not included in the room rate.

## Meals and per diem

The daily meal allowance is 55 EUR for domestic trips and 75 EUR for international trips.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Expense reporting

Expense reports must be submitted in the Expensa tool within 30 days after the end of the trip,
with receipts attached for every item above 25 EUR. Late reports are reimbursed only after
approval from the finance department.
